<h3>About the course</h3><p>An introductory course on how individuals, firms, and governments make decisions under scarcity. It covers supply and demand, prices, markets, incentives, and efficiency, and uses them to understand everyday economic phenomena such as price changes, wages, and taxation. It also introduces basic game theory.</p>
